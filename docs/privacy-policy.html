Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 26, 2026 · Last updated: November 26, 2026

At a glance

  • No account. CSE Prep PH does not require a name, email address, or sign-up.
  • Your progress stays on your phone. Attempts, settings, bookmarks, daily results, and content reports are stored locally.
  • Ads are provided by Google AdMob. The app currently requests non-personalized ads. Google may process device and ad-interaction data under its own policy.
  • No sale of personal data. We do not sell your information.
  • No server for your study data. There is no account database or cloud sync.
  • You are in control. Reset progress, clear reports, or uninstall the app to remove local data.

1. Who this policy is for

CSE Prep PH is an independent, offline-first reviewer for the Philippine Civil Service Examination. It is developed and operated by an independent developer in the Philippines. This policy explains what happens to information when you use the Android app and this website.

Contact: nikkorod04@gmail.com

2. Information stored on your device

The app stores the following locally using on-device storage (SQLite and app preferences):

  • Your track (Professional or Subprofessional), exam date, and daily goal.
  • Practice and mock attempts, answers, scores, study time, streaks, and topic accuracy.
  • Bookmarked questions and your error log.
  • Daily challenge completion.
  • Content reports you create (reason and question ID).

This information is used only to run the reviewer on your device. It is not uploaded to a CSE Prep PH server.

3. Advertising and Google AdMob

The app displays ads through Google AdMob. Google and its partners may collect information such as device identifiers (including the advertising ID), IP address, approximate location derived from IP, app interactions, and ad interactions or diagnostics. This information is used to deliver, measure, and improve ads, and is processed under Google's privacy policy.

The app currently requests non-personalized ads. We do not pass your study answers, bookmarks, or reports to AdMob.

Google privacy information: policies.google.com/technologies/ads

If the app later offers an optional paid “Remove ads” unlock, Google Play will process the payment. The app will only receive whether the unlock is active; it will not receive your card or bank details.

4. Content reports and email

If you flag a question, the app stores the report locally and shows it in Profile. When you tap Email report, the app opens your mail app with a prefilled message. Nothing is sent until you tap Send in your mail app.

If you send the report, the developer receives the question ID, section and topic, reason, and the short question text you chose to include. This is used to correct content and is retained as ordinary email correspondence.

5. Notifications

If you enable the daily reminder, the app schedules a local notification on your device. Reminder settings stay on the device. There is no push-notification server.

6. What we do not collect

  • No account, name, email address, phone number, or contacts.
  • No precise GPS location.
  • No access to your photos, files, microphone, or camera.
  • No study answers, bookmarks, or reports sent to a CSE Prep PH server.
  • No third-party analytics or crash-reporting SDK at this time.

7. Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information is shared only with Google AdMob for advertising as described above, or with your mail provider when you choose to send a content report. Google may process data in other countries under its own safeguards.

8. Retention and deletion

Local study data remains on your device until you delete it. You can:

  • Use Reset progress in Profile to remove attempts, mock results, and streaks.
  • Use Clear reports to remove saved content reports.
  • Uninstall the app to remove all app data from the device.
  • Contact us to ask about email correspondence you sent.

Google retains ad data according to its own policies.

9. Security

Study data is protected by the Android app sandbox on your device. The app does not operate a server that stores your study data. Email reports are transmitted by your own mail provider; their security depends on that provider.

10. Children

CSE Prep PH is intended for people preparing for the Civil Service Examination, generally adults 18 and older. It is not directed to children under 13. If you are under 18, please use the app with a parent or guardian.

11. Your rights under RA 10173

The Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173) gives you rights to be informed, to object, to access, to correct, and to erase personal data, among others. Because the app has no account system and keeps study data on your device, you can exercise most choices directly in the app. For questions or requests about email correspondence, contact nikkorod04@gmail.com.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app changes. The effective date at the top will change, and material updates will be reflected in the app or store listing. Continued use after an update means you accept the revised policy.

13. Disclaimer

CSE Prep PH is an independent reviewer. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to the Civil Service Commission (CSC). All practice items are original. Exam formats, schedules, and coverage can change; always confirm details in the official CSC announcement. The app is a study aid and does not guarantee a passing score. General Information content is for review, not legal advice.
