Verdict

MaterialCommunityIcons is the low-risk move: richer metaphors than Ionicons, zero new dependencies. Phosphor + duotone makes the subject system feel designed and premium; it is the stronger brand choice if you are willing to add one SVG icon package. My pick for CSE Prep: Phosphor, duotone only on the five subject tiles and quick actions, regular weight everywhere else.
